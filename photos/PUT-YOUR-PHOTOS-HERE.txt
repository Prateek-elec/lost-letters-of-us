Put your photos in this folder, for example:
  photos/1.jpg, photos/2.jpg ... photos/12.jpg   (gallery)
  photos/goa.jpg, photos/manali.jpg ...           (map places)

Then open index.html in a text editor (VS Code or Notepad), find the
"gallery:" and "places:" sections near the top, and fill in each
photo:"" like this:   photo:"photos/1.jpg"

Photos only show up when the folder is opened from a website
(Netlify Drop), not by double-clicking index.html. See the steps
in the chat.
