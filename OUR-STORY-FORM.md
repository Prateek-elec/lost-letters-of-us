# Our Story: fill-in form for the game

Fill in what you can, right under each question, and leave a blank wherever you want me to write it
for you in your voice. Short answers are perfect, and you can use English, Hinglish or anything you
two actually talk in. When you're done, just tell me "the form is filled".

**Photos:** put them in the `photos` folder. Name them as suggested (e.g. `gallery-01.jpg`), or just
tell me which file goes where. JPG or PNG are fine and any size works: I'll shrink them so they load
fast on her phone.

> ⚠️ **Privacy:** once this is on Netlify, anyone who has the link can open the game and see the
> photos. Only use pictures you're both comfortable with, and only share the link with her.
