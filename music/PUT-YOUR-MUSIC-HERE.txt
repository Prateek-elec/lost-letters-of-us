Put your songs here (MP3 or M4A files), for example:  our-song.mp3

Then open js/story.js, find the "music" list and add a line for each song:

  music: [
    { title: "Our song", file: "music/our-song.mp3" },
  ],

Optional: to play a special song when the Last Door opens, set
  finaleSong: "music/our-song.mp3",

Tips
- Use simple file names without spaces (our-song.mp3, first-date.mp3).
- Songs play in the order you list them; the jukebox in the meadow skips to the next one.
- The dance floor follows the beat of your song.
- Keep the game link private: songs you bought or downloaded are for your own personal use.
